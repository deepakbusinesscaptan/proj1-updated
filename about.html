Our Story

Fourteen years of watching children find their footing

Beta Juniors began as a single neighbourhood playgroup in Ahmedabad and grew into a preschool brand with a clear conviction: the first six years shape how a child learns for the rest of their life.

Every centre follows the same curriculum, the same safety standards and the same teacher training — so a parent moving cities finds the classroom familiar, and a child finds the routine familiar too.

What We Stand For

Our mission, our promise

A safe second home
