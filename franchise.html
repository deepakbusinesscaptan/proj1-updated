Good To Know

What partners usually ask first

What space do I need?

Most centres run well in 1,500 to 2,500 sq ft on the ground floor, with a safe outdoor or terrace play area and an independent entrance.

Do I need an education background?

No. Many partners come from other fields. What matters is that you are present at the centre and comfortable dealing with parents daily. Academic leadership can be hired, and we help you hire it.

How long until the centre opens?

Typically 10 to 14 weeks from signing, depending on how quickly the space is ready and where you fall in the admissions calendar.

What are the fees and returns?

Investment and royalty depend on city, format and centre size. We share full numbers in the franchise kit after the discovery call — not before, because the ranges are wide enough that a headline figure would mislead.

Is my territory protected?

Yes. Each partner gets an exclusive catchment radius, agreed in writing before setup begins.
