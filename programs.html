Beta Juniors Age-Based Learning Programs

Five programs from 1 to 6 years, each built for what a child can do at that age — and what comes next.

Mother Toddler Program

1–2 Years

A gentle, parent-accompanied start focused on sensory development, bonding and first social contact.

  • ✓Two 90-minute sessions a week
  • ✓Sensory play, rhymes and movement
  • ✓Parent stays through the session

Toddler & Creative Program

2–3 Years

The first independent classroom: routine, sharing, messy play and a lot of talking.

  • ✓Three hours, five days a week
  • ✓Art, water and sand play
  • ✓Toilet-training support

Nursery / Junior KG

3–4 Years

Foundations in language and number, built through stories, songs and hands-on material.

  • ✓Phonics begins
  • ✓Numbers 1–20 and shapes
  • ✓Show-and-tell every Friday

Junior KG / Senior KG

4–5 Years

A balanced mix of academics, creativity and skill-building as school gets closer.

  • ✓Reading simple words
  • ✓Writing practice and fine motor work
  • ✓Group projects and presentations

Senior KG / Balwatika

5–6 Years

Reading, writing, numeracy and life skills that make the move to primary school easy.

  • ✓Sentence reading and writing
  • ✓Addition and subtraction
  • ✓School-readiness assessment
