Before You Write

Quick answers

When do admissions open?

The main cycle opens in January for the April academic year. Mid-year admissions depend on seats in that age group — call and we will tell you what is open today.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Yes, and we recommend it. Weekday mornings between 9:30 and 11:30 let you see a classroom actually running.

What are the day care timings?

Extended day care runs until 6:30pm, including a nap, a hot snack and quiet play.

I want to open a centre, not enrol a child.

Head to the franchise page — the enquiry form there reaches our partnerships team directly.
